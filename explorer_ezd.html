Target Audience: Agency Authorizing Officials (AOs) and their advisors.

This is an analysis of how well NIST SP 800-53 Baselines are addressed by FedRAMP 20x Key Security Indicators (KSIs). It is based on the controls cited by the FedRAMP PMO for each KSI. For more information, see the Analysis Methodology at the bottom of this page.

  • Toggle Agentic AI Support to see how scores can improve when Agentic AI is included in the automation solution.
  • Hover over a control’s Strength rating to see rationale.
  • Hover over a control’s Automatable rating to see prerequisites.

Analysis Methodology

The FedRAMP PMO identifies relevant NIST SP 800-53 controls for each KSI. Each KSI/Control relationship is assessed using the following two criteria:

  • Strength: The KSI’s ability to adequately reflect satisfaction of the control requirement. Hover over each Strength rating to see its rationale.
    Values:
    • Strong (3 points): A clear relationship between the defined KSI and control satisfaction.
    • Fair (2 points): Some relationship exists between the defined KSI and control satisfaction, but it does not provide a complete picture.
    • Weak (1 point): The KSI has a topical relationship to the control, but is a poor indicator of satisfaction.
    • None (0 points): The KSI is not a reflection of control satisfaction.
  • Automatable: The ease of automating KSI evidence collection. Hover over each Automatable rating to see its prerequisites.
    Values:
    • Simple (3 points): Easy queries. Straight-forward data collection.
    • Fair (2 points): More complex queries. Data correlation required.
    • Complex (1 point): Difficult to collect. Complex data correlation. May require human intervention and/or agentic AI support.
    • None (0 points): Unable to collect via traditional automation. May require human intervention or may be possible with agentic AI.

The Automatable determinations initially assumed no agentic AI involvement. A second pass was made to determine how the scores improve when Agentic AI could reasonably contribute to KSI automation. Toggle Agentic AI Support to see how scores change with and without the use of Agentic AI.

Each KSI/Control relationship was then assigned a Score, derived by multiplying the Strength and Automatable ratings.
Values:

  • Excellent (9 points): Automated KSI monitoring provides a highly reliable reflection of control satisfaction.
  • Good (6 points): Automated KSI monitoring provides a reasonable reflection of control satisfaction.
  • Fair (4 points): Automated KSI monitoring provides some reflection of control satisfaction, with gaps.
  • Poor (1–3 points): Automated KSI monitoring provides only a minimal reflection of control satisfaction with major gaps.
  • None (0 points): Automated KSI monitoring is not a reflection of control satisfaction.

Control Coverage:

Controls with two or more KSI relationships (Strength is Weak or better) are further evaluated in the aggregate to determine if a collection of KSI telemetry provides greater control coverage, and an aggregate coverage score is assigned with a rationale.

Controls with exactly one KSI relationship (Strength is Weak or better) are assigned the relationship Score as the Coverage.

Controls with no KSI relationship (Strength is Weak or better) are assigned None as the Coverage.

Family Coverage: The family Score is the average of all control Coverage scores in that family, based on the selected baseline.

Additional Notes
  • Core indicates a legacy FedRAMP Core Control. Sometimes referred to as a Critical Control.
